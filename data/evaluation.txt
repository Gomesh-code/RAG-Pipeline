Evaluating a RAG system means checking two parts separately. Retrieval quality asks whether the correct chunk appears in the top-k results. Common metrics are hit rate and mean reciprocal rank. Generation quality asks whether the final answer is faithful to the retrieved context and actually answers the question.

A simple beginner evaluation is to write 10 questions whose answers you know, run them through the pipeline, and check by hand whether the right source document was retrieved.

Common failure modes include bad chunk sizes, missing documents in the knowledge base, and prompts that do not tell the model to stay within the provided context.
